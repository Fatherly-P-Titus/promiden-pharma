The small icons use the blue mortar-and-pestle and red botanical emblem,
without the wordmark, for better legibility at favicon sizes.

Suggested deployment: place the icon images in frontend/assets/icons/ and
update the HTML head with favicon links and the manifest link. The manifest
paths are written for /assets/icons/. Adjust if your deployment uses another path.

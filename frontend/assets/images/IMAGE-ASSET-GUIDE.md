# Promiden Pharmaceuticals Image Asset Guide

## Image folders
- `frontend/assets/images/products/`: ten generic pharmaceutical image placeholders.
- `frontend/assets/images/services/`: ten realistic-style healthcare/service image placeholders.
- `frontend/assets/images/categories/`: category-level product and service montage images.

## Important
The product images are AI-generated generic placeholders. They do not represent verified Promiden inventory, product labels, dosages, manufacturers, or regulatory approvals. Replace each placeholder with an authorized photograph of the actual product before presenting it as an item for sale. Do not use generated packaging to make product-specific medical or regulatory claims.

Service images are illustrative visuals. Confirm they accurately represent services Promiden actually provides before publishing them.

## Suggested usage
Use individual files for product/service cards. Use category montage images for category landing sections, promotional panels, or fallback thumbnails. Keep image crops consistent in the frontend with `object-fit: cover` and a consistent aspect ratio.

## Actual product photos
Actual photos were not supplied with this update. Add approved photos to the products folder and update the relevant product image paths in the dashboard/database when the content integration milestone is completed.
